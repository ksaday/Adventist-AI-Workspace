# Artificial Intelligence (AI) Disclosure

**Document 44 · Legal & Policy** · v1.1  
**Effective Date:** September 1, 2026  

---

## 1. How Artificial Intelligence Is Used in This Platform

SDA AI Workspace is a workbench designed to safeguard devotional study and sermon preparation against AI fabrication and doctrinal drift.

1. **Our Server Never Calls an AI Model:** Our server does not execute, host, or bill for large language model inference. When you use AI features, the Platform composes protective prompt instructions that you copy into your own personal AI sessions (e.g. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini).
2. **Local Deterministic Processing:** Features such as local prayer drafting (P2), citation detection, canon verification, and five-band segmentation operate deterministically inside your web browser with zero network AI involvement.

---

## 2. Important Limitations of Generative AI

Members and pastors should keep the following limitations in mind:

- **AI Is Not a Minister or Counsellor:** An AI model is a computer program trained on statistical word patterns. It possesses no spiritual discernment, has no personal relationship with God, and cannot speak on behalf of the Holy Spirit.
- **AI Is Prone to Confident Fabrication:** Commercial AI models frequently invent convincing quotations, non-existent Bible verses, and inaccurate page numbers for Ellen G. White writings. An AI output should never be quoted as factual until verified against official primary sources.
- **Agreement Is Not Truth:** Two different AI models agreeing on a quotation does not prove it is real; they may share the same statistical training biases.

---

## 3. Pastoral Responsibility

In **P4 Pastor's Aids**, the Pre-pulpit Citation Checklist strictly blocks marking an outline ready for the pulpit if it contains unverified quotations marked for verbatim preaching. The preacher holds sacred responsibility before God and the congregation for every word uttered from the pulpit. AI tools must remain subordinate aids, never substitutes for pastoral study and prayer.
