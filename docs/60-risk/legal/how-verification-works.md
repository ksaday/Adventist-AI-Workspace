# How Source Verification Works

**Document 45 · Legal & Policy** · v1.1  
**Effective Date:** September 1, 2026  

This document sets forth the rigorous rules governing how SDA AI Workspace validates citations and claims.

---

## 1. The Core Principle: Only E4 Is Verified

In SDA AI Workspace, **only claims at evidence level E4 may ever be shown as verified or in green**.

The Five Rungs:
- **E0 (UNTESTED):** An extracted proposition that has not yet undergone validation.
- **E1 (UNVERIFIED_RECALL):** A quotation or proposition generated by an AI model from statistical recall. Stored as `NOT_VERIFIED`.
- **E2 (MODEL_CONCORDANCE):** A proposition supported by two independent model runs. Does not constitute proof; stored as `NOT_VERIFIED`.
- **E3 (TEXT_CONSISTENT):** A claim whose phrasing is consistent with text supplied by the member during their browser study session. The status is strictly `TEXT_CONSISTENT`, rendered in blue, and never in green.
- **E4 (VERIFIED):** A claim confirmed by the member against an active, official primary source platform. Displayed in green with the status `VERIFIED` and the marker **"Confirmed by you"**.

---

## 2. Why Supplied Text Does Not Equal Official Verification (E3 vs E4)

When you attach source material to an inquiry:
1. **The Supply Channel Is Browser-Only:** Member-supplied source text never reaches our server. Your browser generates a local marker (`client_commitment`) to identify the session block. Our server cannot read or recompute this marker and does not verify the authenticity or provenance of what was pasted.
2. **Consistency, Not Truth:** Our platform can verify that the AI's output is consistent with the supplied text, resulting in level **E3 (`TEXT_CONSISTENT`)**. However, because our server does not authenticate the original document, E3 can never be treated or presented as verified.

---

## 3. Strict Rules for Attestation (Reaching E4)

To advance a claim to E4:
1. **Active Official Host:** The URL must point to an authorized host in our curated Source Directory (e.g. `egwwritings.org` or `documents.adventistarchives.org`).
2. **Deep Reading Passage Required:** The URL must sit strictly under the designated attestation path (e.g. `/read/` or `/Periodicals/`). Bare homepages, search results, and relative paths are strictly rejected.
3. **Actor Ownership:** The attestation must be submitted by the owner of the claim.
